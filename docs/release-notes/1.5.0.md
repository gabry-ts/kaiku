# Kaiku 1.5.0

The floating live window now has Summary and Ask tabs next to the transcript. The summary grows during the call and is saved as live-summary.md when it ends, and you can ask questions about what has been said so far. Both have their own provider and model, chosen in the new Live page in Settings, where the live transcription settings have moved too.

Summaries can now be written by OpenRouter or by the Claude Code, Codex and opencode command line tools you already have installed, and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio joins the transcription providers.

Recordings no longer run for hours after a call. They stop two minutes after the call ends unless you chose otherwise, and the new Forgotten Recordings settings stop any recording after a long silence or past a maximum length, 15 minutes and 4 hours by default. When a recording stops for silence, the silent part at the end is dropped.

A transcription can be cancelled from the library or the menu bar panel. No summary or webhook follows, and the call can then be transcribed again or deleted.

With the microphone on Automatic, Kaiku records from the same microphone as your call and follows it when you switch, for example from the Mac to your headphones in Teams. You can turn this off in Settings > Recording.

Titles taken from Chrome windows no longer include Chrome's camera and microphone notice or your profile name, and the webhook now sends the domains of the other companies invited to the calendar event as external_domains.
