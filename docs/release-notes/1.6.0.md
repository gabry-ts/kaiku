# Kaiku 1.6.0

Kaiku now has one window. Calls, Chat and Settings sit in the same sidebar, ⌘, takes you straight to Settings, and Settings were rebuilt from scratch: a search field that jumps to the right row, a Needs Attention list at the top, and every API key in one Accounts page with a Test Key button.

You can chat with your calls. Pick a few, a tag, a source or a period and ask away. Answers stream in and their citations jump to that moment in the audio. It works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, Claude Code, Codex and opencode, and now also with Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server running on your Mac, which you can use for summaries and Live Assist too.

The transcript reads better: bigger text you can resize with ⌘+ and ⌘−, a centered or full-width layout, and the word being said is highlighted while the call plays. Double-click a word to play from there. With whisper.cpp the word timings are now aligned to the audio, so the highlight keeps up.

Audio and video files can be imported. Drop them on the window, on the menu bar icon or on the Dock, or use ⌘O, and they are transcribed like any call.

Dictation is new and off until you turn it on in Settings. Hold or tap a shortcut anywhere, talk, and the text is typed where your cursor is. It works with the local or cloud provider you prefer, and can be cleaned up by an AI model with your own modes, like Email or Message.

Recording can start by itself only for the sources you choose. Speaker names come from the calendar invite when the call had just one other person. Action items from the summary can be sent to Reminders, Things or Linear, and go out with the webhook. Smart search finds passages by meaning, on your Mac.

Agents can read your calls: Settings adds Kaiku's MCP server to Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop in one click. There are Shortcuts actions, calls show up in Spotlight, and a Raycast extension is on its way to the Raycast Store.

Mute all microphones now lowers the input volume to 1% by default, so Teams keeps the microphone. The live whisper transcription keeps its model loaded instead of reloading it every few seconds, which uses far less battery, and the mix you listen to balances your voice and the call. Lots of fixes too: ⌘Q closes the window instead of quitting, the panel no longer jumps when muting, back-to-back recordings no longer interfere, and edits made during a transcription are kept.
