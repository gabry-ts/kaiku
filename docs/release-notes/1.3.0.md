# Kaiku 1.3.0

Kaiku can now show the transcript while you talk. Turn on Live Transcription in Settings > General and the text appears during the call, labelled Me and Them, in the popover and in a floating window you can keep on top. It runs on your Mac with the system speech recognizer, needs macOS 26, and downloads the language model once when you switch it on. After the call you choose whether the live text is only a preview, replaced by the usual transcription, or is kept as the transcript. It is off until you turn it on.

The new Notifications page in Settings lists every notification Kaiku sends, each with its own switch and its own sound switch. Your current choices carry over.

You can also choose what happens when a call ends: stop after a delay as before, ask every time and keep recording until you answer, or do nothing.

The Settings window now looks like a macOS 26 sidebar window, with the close, minimize and zoom buttons inside the sidebar.
